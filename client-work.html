Memory Matters
Nonprofit brain health organization · Hilton Head Island, SC · mymemorymatters.org

Completely rebuilt and relaunched the site for a nationally recognized dementia care and brain health nonprofit serving the South Carolina Lowcountry. Took over a disorganized existing site and delivered a polished, professional rebuild. Then, stayed on as the sole developer maintaining and expanding it for over two years. The site handles a wide range of real-world nonprofit needs: multi-program listings, event calendars, newsletter signup, donation flows, caregiver support resources, a member portal, and multiple location pages. Built on Wix Studio with custom Velo/JavaScript for dynamic behavior beyond what the visual editor supports.

→ Still their developer today, over two years after the original rebuild shipped, they've never needed to look for anyone else.

Big T's Bakery
Full-stack e-commerce site · Thailand

Built a full-stack bilingual (EN/TH) bakery e-commerce site for a young entrepreneur in Thailand. Features a dynamic menu loaded from a Postgres database, a live cart with custom icing colour surcharges, a weekend-only date picker, and a payment slip upload flow using Vercel Blob. Orders hit an admin dashboard (Clerk-protected) with inline status management, full order detail views, and a menu CMS for adding new products. Notification emails fire via Resend on every order. Deployed on Vercel.
